Timing and background behavior

Foreground only alternates on/off every 500 ms while Safari runs the page. Safari can slow or pause this timer when the page is hidden.

Background continuity gives beacio the repeating sequence. It targets 2 seconds on, 2 seconds off, while the page is visible or hidden. Returning to Safari doesn’t stop it. Stop ends the sequence and sends LED off.

The 2-second minimum is beacio’s current conservative battery/resource policy, rather than an iOS-imposed minimum. Sustained 500 ms app operation hasn’t been validated or enabled. App timing is best effort and needs a live Bluetooth connection and a running native session.

The indicator shows the emulated peripheral’s last reported value. The page can’t display live changes while its JavaScript is paused. A read refreshes it when you return. Closing or reloading the tab, ending the native session, or losing the connection can interrupt continuity.
