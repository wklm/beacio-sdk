Blink a Bluetooth LED, then lock your phone.

A blink program from your last visit is still running. It keeps writing to your LED until you stop it.

    Step by step

    1. 1

      Open the beacio app and leave it running.

      You see the beacio app. Come back here.

    2. 2

      Tap Connect and choose your LED.

      You see the lamp say Connected, On or Off.

    3. 3

      Pick who keeps it blinking.

      You see your choice ticked.

    4. 4

      Tap Start blinking.

      You see the LED and the lamp blink.

    5. 5

      Leave Safari or lock the phone.

      Watch the LED. Foreground only: it slows, then stops. Background continuity: it keeps blinking every 2 s.

    6. 6

      Come back to this page.

      You see the lamp catch up, still blinking.

    7. 7

      Tap Stop.

      You see the LED turn off if connected. Leaving the page never stops a background program.

    Who does what

    In Foreground only, the page sends every blink itself, through beacio. In Background continuity, it hands the beacio app one small program, and the app keeps it going.

    Why the page alone stops

    Good to know

    • 2 s on, 2 s off. 2000 ms is the time between commands and beacio’s current minimum, not an iOS limit. A faster program is rejected, not slowed down.
    • Coming back doesn’t stop it. Only Stop does: it unregisters the program, then the page sends off if it is connected.
    • Best effort. If iOS ends the beacio app or the Bluetooth link drops, blinks can be late or missed.
    • Twice is fine. While beacio runs, a notified value can arrive twice. The lamp shows the value, so it stays right.
